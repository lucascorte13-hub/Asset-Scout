Adding Assets: Select a market (Crypto, US, BR Stock, BR Fund), type the symbol, and click Add. The price will load automatically.

Entry Price: Click directly on the Entry Price cell in the table to set the price you consider a good buy. This is the price level you want to enter a position at.

Target Price: Click directly on the Target Price cell to set your sell/exit target. This is the price you expect the asset to reach.

Discount Column: Shows how far below your Entry Price the current price is. A negative value (yellow) means the asset is trading below your entry — a potential buying opportunity. Click the column header to sort assets by discount.

Upside Column: Shows the potential gain from the current price to your Target Price. Click the column header to sort assets by upside potential.

Green row glow 🟢: When the current price drops below your Entry Price, the row lights up green to signal a buying opportunity.

White row glow ⚪: When the current price reaches or exceeds your Target Price, the row lights up white to signal a sell/take profit opportunity.

Red row glow 🔴: When the price is simultaneously below Entry Price and above Target Price, it means the values were entered incorrectly. Review and correct your Entry/Target values.

Tips: Use Expand All / Collapse All to quickly navigate. Drag windows and columns in Settings to customize your layout. All data is saved automatically.
